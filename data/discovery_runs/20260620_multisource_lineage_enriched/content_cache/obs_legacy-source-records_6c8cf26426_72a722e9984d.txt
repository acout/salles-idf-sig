MJC maison associations location salle Gentilly — # Maison des Jeunes et de la Culture. The Maison des Jeunes et de la Culture and its performance hall "La Coupole" play a major role in Chamonix's socio-cultural life. The Maison des Jeunes et de la Culture is an association under the law of 1901, with a non profit goal and popular education, approved by the State to welcome children and young people between 4 and 17 years old. Within the MJC, the theater la Coupole offers a rich and varied artistic and cultural menu for all tastes. Through a musical season, it does not hesitate to play the card of eclecticism and the pleasure of giving to hear, to listen, to see, to enjoy, to share, to practice, to participate, to organize... La Coupole's action is not limited to the diffusion of professional concerts. All year round on Tuesday between 1.30 pm and 6 pm.
